How to play

Basics

  • Black and White take turns placing one stone on an intersection. Black goes first.
  • Stones never move. They can only be captured.
  • Goal: control more of the board than your opponent.

Capture

  • A liberty is an empty point directly next to a stone (not diagonal).
  • Touching stones of one colour form a group and share liberties.
  • A group with no liberties is captured and removed.
  • A group with one liberty left is in atari.

Illegal moves

  • Suicide: you can't leave your own group with no liberties, unless the move captures.
  • Ko: you can't repeat an earlier board position.

Ending and scoring

  • Press Pass instead of playing. Two passes in a row end the game.
  • Click groups that can't survive to mark them dead, then press Finish scoring.
  • Score = your living stones + empty points only you surround. White also gets komi.

Tips

  • A group with two separate eyes can't be captured.
  • Claim corners first, then sides, then the centre.
Rules: area (Chinese) scoring, positional superko, no suicide. After two passes, click groups to mark them dead, then finish scoring.
